`scripts/art/generate-images.mjs`; regenerate with `npm run images`). They are procedural canvas illustrations — gradients, shapes, noise grain and
simple lighting — of **fictional, unbranded objects** (bags, knitwear, ceramics, candles, lamps, etc.). They contain:

- no third-party photographs, stock images, screenshots or scans;
- no logos, trademarks, brand names or imitations of any real product or luxury brand;
- no output from an AI image model;
- no people or faces.

They are owned by OneStop Infinite and supplied under the project LICENSE for use in projects built from this template.
They are **illustrations, not photography**: a real business should replace them with its own licensed product photography
(see "Replacing images" in `CUSTOMIZATION_GUIDE.md`).
