- **Redistribution:** the Unsplash License does not allow selling or redistributing the photos on their own or compiling them into a competing image service.

  photograph with your own or your client's photography (CUSTOMIZATION_GUIDE.md → Replacing images).
- **People / model releases:** several photographs show identifiable people training or in an ice bath (`prog-hiit`, `prog-personal`, `about-community`, `fac-plunge`, `fac-group`, `intro-lifter`,
  `prog-mobility`). The Unsplash License gives **no model release** and does not permit implying that those people endorse a business, product or any trainer named on the site.
  The fictional trainers are *not* these people. Replace with consented photography before launch.
- **Trademarks / property:** some photographs incidentally show equipment brands, plate markings or gym branding (e.g. machines in `hero-main` / `about-floor`, plates in `prog-strength`, `philosophy-plates`,
  `cta-stripes`). None is used as a logo or endorsement; remove or replace if a client's trademark policy requires it. I chose not to use one photograph whose brand was prominent.
- Photos depict real facilities or equipment but carry no claim that they show a VIGOR CLUB location; the site's demo notice says so.
- Licence terms can change. Re-check https://unsplash.com/license before distribution. Photographer credit is not required.
